Every launcher update, newest first. Installed launchers update themselves.

Launcher 0.6.18

LATESTNovember 8, 2026
  • Points coin · Points now have their own gold coin in the Locker, on prices and in the daily deal.

Launcher 0.6.17

November 8, 2026
  • Daily deal · One cosmetic is 30% off every day, at the top of the Locker and on the cosmetics page.
  • Daily reward · Claim points every day in the game's main menu. Up to 25 a day, and more every week you keep your streak. Miss a day and it starts over.
  • Leaderboards · New Points tabs and Top inviters of the month on the players page, and profiles show points and unlocked items (you can hide them in the client's settings).

Launcher 0.6.16

November 8, 2026
  • Invite button · Copy your invite link straight from the Friends page or the Locker.

Launcher 0.6.15

November 8, 2026
  • Points and unlocks · Earn points by playing, with a daily streak bonus and by inviting friends, then unlock wings, halos, hats, auras and capes in the Locker. A few capes stay free, and anything you already wore stays yours.
  • Invite friends · Share comclient.xyz/r/your-name: once your friend has played an hour, you both get points.

Launcher 0.6.14

November 8, 2026
  • Crashes explained · When Minecraft crashes, the popup says what most likely went wrong and what to do, in plain words.
  • Quick tour · New players get three short steps on the first start: sign in, pick a profile, open the Locker.

Launcher 0.6.13

November 8, 2026

Launcher 0.6.0

November 7, 2026
  • Gallery · Every screenshot from all your profiles in one grid: open, copy, share or delete them.
  • Replays · Your ReplayMod recordings, with a Watch button that starts the game straight into one.
  • Friends · Who's online and on which server, Join them with one click, and chat right from the launcher.
  • Modpacks · Mods Modpacks: install a whole Modrinth modpack as a new profile in one click.

Launcher 0.5.1

November 7, 2026

Launcher 0.5.0

November 7, 2026
  • Profiles · A new Profiles page: names, Minecraft block or item icons (or your own picture), duplicate, export and import.
  • Quick join · Pin servers to a profile. They sit next to PLAY: one click starts the game and joins.
  • Play time · Total hours, time per profile, your last sessions and the past two weeks.
  • Custom colour · Pick any accent colour you like in Settings Appearance.
  • Tray · While you play, the launcher waits in the system tray and comes back when you quit.
  • Sounds · Soft click and hover sounds, the same as in game. Switch them off in Settings.
