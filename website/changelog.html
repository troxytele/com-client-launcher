Changelog

What's new

Every launcher update, newest first. Installed launchers update themselves.

Launcher 0.6.6

LATESTNovember 8, 2026
  • New update screen · A progress ring, download speed, time left and what's new while the launcher updates, and Try again if something goes wrong.
  • Discord · Your status has a Download Com Client button.

Launcher 0.6.5

November 8, 2026
  • Wings fixed · In the Locker the wings now sit on your back and spread out like in the game.

Launcher 0.6.4

November 8, 2026
  • Starts faster · The game files are checked and downloaded in the background, and files that were already checked aren't read again, so Play starts right away.
  • Mod updates · When your mods, resource packs or shaders have updates, the Mods tab shows how many and the launch bar has an Update all button.

Launcher 0.6.3

November 8, 2026
  • Locker looks like the game · Wings are held out to the sides and beat like in Com Client, and every wing, halo, hat and aura card shows the same picture as in the game.

Launcher 0.6.2

November 8, 2026
  • Locker fixed · Cape names and pictures show properly again, and picking one of the official capes works.
  • Friends fixed · Signing in to Com Client no longer fails when the page loads or while the game is running.

Launcher 0.6.1

November 7, 2026
  • Logs window · A separate window with the game's log (colours, filters, search), its memory and CPU, and one-click upload to mclo.gs. Turn it on in Settings, or open it from the Logs page.

Launcher 0.6.0

November 7, 2026
  • Gallery · Every screenshot from all your profiles in one grid: open, copy, share or delete them.
  • Replays · Your ReplayMod recordings, with a Watch button that starts the game straight into one.
  • Friends · Who's online and on which server, Join them with one click, and chat right from the launcher.
  • Modpacks · Mods → Modpacks: install a whole Modrinth modpack as a new profile in one click.
  • Locker · Browse and wear capes, wings, halos, hats and auras, tried on your own skin in 3D. All free for now.
  • Auto-optimise · On the Profiles page: checks your PC and picks the memory, performance mods and video settings.

Launcher 0.5.1

November 7, 2026
  • Rename profiles · Click a profile's name (or Rename) on the Profiles page, or double-click it in the list, then type the new name.

Launcher 0.5.0

November 7, 2026
  • Profiles · A new Profiles page: names, Minecraft block or item icons (or your own picture), duplicate, export and import.
  • Quick join · Pin servers to a profile. They sit next to PLAY: one click starts the game and joins.
  • Play time · Total hours, time per profile, your last sessions and the past two weeks.
  • Custom colour · Pick any accent colour you like in Settings → Appearance.
  • Tray · While you play, the launcher waits in the system tray and comes back when you quit.
  • Sounds · Soft click and hover sounds, the same as in game. Switch them off in Settings.

Earlier versions

0.1 – 0.4
  • The first launcher · Microsoft sign-in with several accounts, automatic Minecraft, Java and Fabric install, the Modrinth browser, skins, crash helper and automatic updates.
